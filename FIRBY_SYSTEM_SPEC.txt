1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Firby is a hyper-realistic, autonomous 24/7 AI Commercial Advisor and Smart
Receptionist engineered specifically for businesses (clinics, medical centers,
aesthetic centers, SaaS, and service businesses) operating on WhatsApp.

Unlike generic chatbots or rigid decision-tree bots:
- Firby operates 100% dynamically via LLMs (Groq Llama-3.3-70b / Gemini 2.5 Flash).
- She speaks with natural Latin/Colombian warmth, behaving like a real, empathetic
  commercial advisor named Valeria (or any custom tenant name like Andrea/Lucía).
- She uses native WhatsApp formatting (zero inverted '¿' or '¡' marks, natural
  emojis, short multi-bubble messages).
- She implements WhatsApp Conversion Psychology (3rd Bubble Isolated Closing
  Questions and Behavioral Mirroring for text and voice notes).
- She runs a zero-RAM-bloat file-system persistent memory architecture per tenant.


2. CORE FEATURE MATRIX & ARCHITECTURAL CAPABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[F-01] NATIVE HUMAN WHATSAPP FORMATTING ENGINE (firby_nuke_robot_phrases.py)
       - Strips all inverted opening punctuation ('¿' and '¡') globally from
         100% of LLM text outputs. Humans on WhatsApp use single trailing marks ('?').
       - Eliminates robotic identity clichés ("Hola, soy Firby la IA recepcionista").
       - Replaces cold introductions with warm advisor openings:
         "Holaa! Hablas con Valeria de {clinic_name}, qué rico saludarte!"

[F-02] ISOLATED 3RD BUBBLE CLOSING QUESTION TECHNIQUE
       - Automatically splits outgoing response bubbles separated by '|||'.
       - Isolates the closing question into its own standalone 3rd bubble
         (e.g., "Prefieres jornada de la mañana o de la tarde?").
       - Positioned directly above the lead's keyboard input box on WhatsApp,
         boosting lead reply rates by over +40%.

[F-03] PSYCHOLOGY OF BEHAVIORAL MIRRORING (ESPEJEO EN AUDIO Y TEXTO)
       - When a lead requests a voice note or asks about a treatment
         ("mandame una nota de voz explicándome el Botox"):
         Firby DOES NOT give meta explanations ("puedo enviar audios").
       - She IMMEDIATELY mirrors the lead's emotional tone and topic in character:
         "Mira te cuento cómo trabajamos eso: aplicamos el producto en puntos
         estratégicos para relajar el músculo sin perder expresión natural."
       - Renders voice notes in real-time via ElevenLabs audio engine.

[F-04] MULTI-TENANT IDENTITY & CUSTOM AGENT NAMING
       - 100% tenant isolation. If Tenant A configures the name "Andrea",
         Firby updates IDENTITY.md and runtime prompts for Tenant A ONLY.
       - Tenant B retains its own custom identity ("Valeria", "Lucía", "Sofia").
       - Prompt getters dynamically bind the tenant's agent name across all
         greetings, audio notes, and admin reports.

[F-05] STRUCTURED ADMIN ONBOARDING INTERVIEW ENGINE (/onboarding)
       - Conducts a multi-step interactive onboarding interview with the founder/admin:
         1. Founder / Doctor Identity (Dra. Carolina Gómez, Dr. Alejandro, team size).
         2. Clinic Specialty & Star Treatments.
         3. Night & After-Hours Policy (Soft Guidance to 8 AM vs 24/7 Auto Booking).
         4. Admin Notification WhatsApp Number.
       - Stores onboarding results persistently in instance files:
         /instances/{instance_id}/identity/IDENTITY.md
         /instances/{instance_id}/soul/SOUL.md
         /instances/{instance_id}/knowledge/teachings.json

[F-06] FILE-BASED INFINITE MEMORY STORE (ZERO AWS RAM BLOAT)
       - To prevent RAM consumption on AWS servers and avoid LLM token context exhaustion:
         * Tenant Identity: /instances/{id}/identity/IDENTITY.md
         * Business Soul & Learnings: /instances/{id}/soul/SOUL.md
         * Knowledge & Pricing Base: /instances/{id}/knowledge/teachings.json
         * Patient History: /instances/{id}/memory/chats/{chat_id}.json
       - Before generating any candidate response, Firby reads these files
         from disk in milliseconds.

[F-07] TIME-AWARE AFTER-HOURS INTELLIGENCE (3:00 AM MADRUGADA)
       - Calculates local time in Colombia (UTC-5).
       - When a lead writes late at night or 3:00 AM:
         * Soft Guidance Mode (Default): Answers all questions warmly, then
           clarifies gently that final medical appointment slots are confirmed
           at 8:00 AM by the team.
         * 24/7 Auto-Booking Mode (Admin Override): Processes full instant
           booking at any hour if enabled by the admin.

[F-08] REAL-TIME ADMIN ↔ CLIENT NOTIFICATION ENGINE (AdminOutreachEngine)
       - When a lead books an appointment or needs human assistance, Firby
         sends a natural, warm WhatsApp report to her boss (the Admin):
         "Hola! Te cuento que acabo de agendar a Camila Restrepo (3015218242)
          para Botox el Viernes 3:00 PM! Quedó guardada en la agenda.
          Si quieres enviarle un mensaje desde aquí, dime o escribe 'háblale a 3015218242'."
       - Supports proactive outbound messages: /escribir <number>, háblale a <number>.

[F-09] SURGICAL LLM PROMPT REFINEMENT ENGINE (_refine_prompt_section_via_llm)
       - When the admin requests a tone or style adjustment ("quiero que hables
         de forma más ejecutiva y lujosa"):
       - Firby invokes an internal LLM Prompt Architect to update ONLY the custom
         tone section in personas/{instance_id}/runtime_override.json without
         destroying existing commercial rules or booking constraints.

[F-10] MULTI-PROVIDER RESILIENT LLM FALLBACK CASCADE
       - Primary: Groq (Llama-3.3-70b-versatile)
       - Backup Series: Gemini 2.5 Flash (7 rotated API keys)
       - Fallback: OpenAI / Claude
       - Automatic 429 rate limit detection and 180s circuit breaker locking.

[F-11] OMNICHANNEL & CALENDAR INTEGRATIONS
       - WhatsApp: Baileys WebSockets Bridge (resolve JID via sock.onWhatsApp).
       - Google Calendar: OAuth2 direct binding, slot availability, event creation.
       - Notion / Web Search: SerpAPI Google search & Notion workspace sync.
